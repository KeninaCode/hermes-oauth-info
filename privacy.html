Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Overview

Hermes Gmail Reader is a personal desktop application intended for private use by its owner.

Google Account Data

The application accesses Google account data only with the account owner's authorization and for the owner's personal workflows.

Data Storage

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer. Google account data is processed as required for the application's functionality.

Data Sharing

The application is not offered to third-party users and does not sell personal data.

Data Deletion

The owner can revoke access through Google Account settings and remove locally stored application data.
